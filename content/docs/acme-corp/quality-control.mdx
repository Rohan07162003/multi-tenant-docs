# Quality Control

Acme Corp maintains rigorous quality standards across all production lines to ensure customer satisfaction and regulatory compliance.

## Quality Standards

### ISO Certifications
- ISO 9001:2015 - Quality Management Systems
- ISO 14001:2015 - Environmental Management
- ISO 45001:2018 - Occupational Health and Safety

### Industry Standards
- ANSI/ASME standards for industrial equipment
- UL certification for electrical products
- CE marking for European market compliance

## Inspection Procedures

### Incoming Materials
1. **Visual Inspection**: Check for damage, defects
2. **Dimensional Verification**: Measure critical dimensions
3. **Material Testing**: Verify material properties
4. **Documentation Review**: Confirm certificates and specs

### In-Process Quality Control
- Real-time monitoring via sensors
- Statistical process control (SPC)
- Operator quality checks every 30 minutes
- Automated rejection of out-of-spec products

### Final Product Testing
- Functional testing (100% of products)
- Safety testing per applicable standards
- Packaging inspection
- Random batch testing for extended validation

## Quality Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators
- **First Pass Yield**: 96.5% (Target: >95%)
- **Customer Return Rate**: 0.2% (Target: < 0.5%)
- **Audit Score**: 98% (Target: >95%)
- **Supplier Quality Rating**: 99.1% (Target: >98%)

## Corrective Actions

When quality issues are identified:
1. Immediate containment
2. Root cause analysis
3. Corrective action implementation
4. Verification of effectiveness
5. Documentation and training updates
