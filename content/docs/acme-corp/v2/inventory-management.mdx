# Inventory Management

Acme Corp's inventory management system ensures optimal stock levels while minimizing carrying costs and preventing stockouts.

## Warehouse Locations

### Primary Warehouse (Chicago)
- **Size**: 200,000 sq ft
- **Capacity**: 50,000 SKUs
- **Automation**: Automated storage and retrieval system (AS/RS)
- **Staff**: 45 warehouse personnel

### Regional Distribution Centers
- **East Coast** (Atlanta): 75,000 sq ft
- **West Coast** (Los Angeles): 80,000 sq ft  
- **International** (Rotterdam): 60,000 sq ft

## Inventory Categories

### Raw Materials
- Steel and metal components
- Electronic components
- Packaging materials
- Chemicals and lubricants

### Work in Progress (WIP)
- Sub-assemblies
- Partially completed products
- Quality hold items

### Finished Goods
- Consumer products ready for shipment
- Industrial equipment
- Spare parts and accessories

## Inventory Control Methods

### ABC Analysis
- **A Items** (20% of items, 80% of value): Daily cycle counts
- **B Items** (30% of items, 15% of value): Weekly cycle counts  
- **C Items** (50% of items, 5% of value): Monthly cycle counts

### Reorder Points
Calculated using:
- Lead time demand
- Safety stock levels
- Supplier reliability factors

### Technology Integration
- RFID tracking for high-value items
- Barcode scanning for all transactions
- Real-time inventory visibility
- Automated reorder triggers
