# Transportation Management

GlobalDyne Industries operates a comprehensive transportation network combining owned fleet, contracted carriers, and strategic partnerships to ensure reliable, cost-effective delivery services worldwide.

## Fleet Overview

### Owned Fleet Assets
- **Trucks**: 2,500 vehicles (Class 5-8)
- **Trailers**: 8,000 units (dry van, refrigerated, flatbed)
- **Aircraft**: 12 cargo planes (Boeing 767F, 747F)
- **Ocean Vessels**: 5 container ships in partnership
- **Last-Mile Vehicles**: 1,200 delivery vans and trucks

### Geographic Coverage
- **North America**: 100% coverage with 2-day standard delivery
- **Europe**: 95% coverage with 3-day standard delivery  
- **Asia-Pacific**: 85% coverage with 5-day standard delivery
- **Latin America**: 70% coverage with 7-day standard delivery

## Transportation Modes

### Ground Transportation

#### Long-Haul Operations
- **Route Optimization**: AI-powered routing for fuel efficiency
- **Driver Management**: Electronic logs and hours-of-service compliance
- **Maintenance**: Preventive maintenance every 10,000 miles
- **Fuel Management**: Fleet cards with real-time monitoring

#### Regional Distribution
- **Hub-and-Spoke Model**: 15 major hubs with spoke networks
- **Cross-Docking**: 85% of shipments cross-dock within 4 hours
- **Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)**: Consolidated shipping for efficiency
- **Full-Truckload (FTL)**: Direct shipping for large volumes

#### Last-Mile Delivery
- **Urban Centers**: Same-day and next-day delivery options
- **Suburban Areas**: Standard 1-2 day delivery
- **Rural Areas**: 2-3 day delivery with consolidated routes
- **Special Handling**: White glove and appointment delivery services

### Air Transportation

#### Express Services
- **Next-Day Air**: Guaranteed delivery by 10:30 AM
- **2-Day Air**: Guaranteed delivery by end of day
- **International Express**: 1-3 day delivery to major cities
- **Critical Shipments**: Same-day air charter available

#### Air Network Hubs
- **Primary Hub**: Atlanta (ATL) - 24/7 operations
- **Secondary Hubs**: Chicago (ORD), Los Angeles (LAX)
- **International Gateways**: Miami (MIA), New York (JFK)

- **Full Container Load (FCL)**: 20ft and 40ft containers
