# Transportation v1.0

## Overview

This document covers the basic transportation and fleet management operations used in GlobalDyne Industries' v1.0 system.

## Fleet Composition (v1.0)

### Light Duty Vehicles
- **Vans**: 80 vehicles
- **Pickup Trucks**: 40 vehicles
- **Capacity**: Up to 2 tons
- **Range**: Local delivery (50 miles)

### Heavy Duty Vehicles
- **Box Trucks**: 60 vehicles
- **Semi Trucks**: 20 vehicles
- **Capacity**: Up to 40 tons
- **Range**: Regional delivery (500 miles)

## Route Management

### Planning Process
1. Manual route planning
2. Paper-based delivery schedules
3. Driver briefings
4. Basic GPS navigation

### Delivery Operations
- Fixed delivery windows
- Manual proof of delivery
- Paper-based tracking
- Phone-based communication

## Vehicle Maintenance

### Scheduled Maintenance
- Oil changes every 5,000 miles
- Tire rotation quarterly
- Annual inspections
- Paper-based maintenance logs

### Repairs
- In-house mechanics for basic repairs
- External service for major repairs
- Parts inventory management
- Downtime tracking

## Driver Management

### Requirements
- Commercial Driver's License (CDL)
- Clean driving record
- Annual safety training
- Drug and alcohol testing

### Performance Tracking
- Delivery completion rates
- Fuel efficiency monitoring
- Safety incident reporting
- Customer feedback

## Communication

### Dispatch System
- Radio communication
- Phone-based updates
- Paper dispatch sheets
- End-of-day reporting

## Performance Metrics (v1.0)

- **On-Time Delivery**: 85% target
- **Fuel Efficiency**: 8 MPG average
- **Vehicle Utilization**: 75% target
- **Safety Rating**: Zero accidents goal

## Legacy Notes

This v1.0 transportation system provided our operational foundation. The v2.0 system includes GPS tracking, real-time routing, telematics, and automated dispatch.
