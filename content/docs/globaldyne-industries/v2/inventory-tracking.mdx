# Inventory Tracking

GlobalDyne Industries employs advanced inventory tracking technologies to provide real-time visibility across the entire supply chain, from supplier facilities to customer delivery.

## Tracking Technologies

### RFID Technology
- **Ultra-High Frequency (UHF)**: Long-range reading for pallets and cases
- **High Frequency (HF)**: Item-level tracking for high-value products  
- **Passive Tags**: Cost-effective for most inventory items
- **Active Tags**: GPS-enabled for shipment tracking

### Barcode Systems
- **Linear Barcodes**: Standard UPC/EAN for retail products
- **2D Barcodes**: Data Matrix and QR codes for complex data
- **GS1 Standards**: Globally recognized identification standards
- **Batch and Lot Tracking**: Expiration date and quality control

### IoT Sensors
- **Temperature Monitoring**: Cold chain integrity for pharmaceuticals
- **Humidity Sensors**: Environmental control for sensitive goods
- **Shock Sensors**: Impact detection for fragile items
- **GPS Trackers**: Real-time location for high-value shipments

### Inventory Categories

#### By Velocity
- **Fast Movers**: >100 transactions/month (15% of SKUs, 60% of volume)
- **Medium Movers**: 20-100 transactions/month (25% of SKUs, 30% of volume)
- **Slow Movers**: 5-20 transactions/month (35% of SKUs, 8% of volume)
- **Dead Stock**: < 5 transactions/month (25% of SKUs, 2% of volume)

#### By Value Classification
- **Class A**: High-value items (20% of SKUs, 80% of value)
- **Class B**: Medium-value items (30% of SKUs, 15% of value)
- **Class C**: Low-value items (50% of SKUs, 5% of value)

#### Special Handling Categories
- **Hazardous Materials**: Special storage and handling requirements
- **Temperature-Controlled**: Cold chain and climate-controlled items
- **High Security**: Valuable items requiring additional protection
- **Serialized Items**: Individual unit tracking for warranties

## Tracking Workflows

### Inbound Tracking
1. **ASN (Advance Ship Notice)**: Electronic notification from supplier
2. **Receiving Scan**: Barcode/RFID capture at dock door
3. **Quality Check**: Inspection and condition verification
4. **Put-Away**: Location assignment and system update
5. **Inventory Update**: Real-time availability for allocation

### Inventory Movements
- **Transfers**: Between locations within same facility
- **Relocations**: Moves to optimize pick efficiency  
- **Adjustments**: Cycle count corrections and write-offs
- **Reservations**: Allocation for pending orders
- **Holds**: Quality or compliance-related inventory blocks

### Outbound Tracking
1. **Pick Assignment**: Wave planning and pick list generation
2. **Pick Confirmation**: Scan verification during picking
3. **Pack Verification**: Item and quantity confirmation
4. **Ship Scan**: Final scan before carrier pickup
5. **Delivery Confirmation**: Proof of delivery capture

## Cycle Counting Program

### Count Frequency by Classification
- **Class A Items**: Weekly counts (52 times per year)
- **Class B Items**: Monthly counts (12 times per year)
- **Class C Items**: Quarterly counts (4 times per year)
- **Special Items**: Daily counts for high-risk inventory

### Count Methodologies
- **Perpetual Counting**: Continuous cycle counts throughout year
- **Random Sampling**: Statistical sampling for large populations
- **Exception-Based**: Counts triggered by system discrepancies
- **Physical Inventory**: Annual wall-to-wall count for validation

### Count Accuracy Targets
- **Overall Accuracy**: >99.5%
- **A-Item Accuracy**: >99.9%
- **B-Item Accuracy**: >99.7%
- **C-Item Accuracy**: >99.0%

## Demand Forecasting

### Forecasting Models
- **Time Series Analysis**: Historical pattern recognition
- **Seasonal Adjustment**: Holiday and seasonal demand patterns
- **Trend Analysis**: Growth and decline pattern identification
- **Collaborative Planning**: Customer forecast integration

### Forecast Accuracy Metrics
- **Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE)**: < 15% target
- **Bias**: Tracking over/under forecasting trends
- **Forecast Value Added (FVA)**: Improvement over statistical baseline

### Replenishment Triggers
- **Reorder Points**: Minimum stock levels for automatic replenishment
- **Safety Stock**: Buffer inventory for demand variability
- **Lead Time**: Supplier delivery time considerations
- **Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)**: Optimal order sizing

## Exception Management

### System Alerts
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Inventory below reorder point
- **Overstock Alerts**: Inventory exceeding maximum levels
- **Aging Inventory**: Slow-moving stock alerts
- **Discrepancy Alerts**: Count variance notifications

### Exception Resolution Process
1. **Alert Generation**: Automated system notification
2. **Investigation**: Root cause analysis
3. **Corrective Action**: Process or system adjustment
4. **Verification**: Confirmation of resolution
5. **Documentation**: Case closure and lessons learned
