# Supplier Portal

The GlobalDyne Supplier Portal is a comprehensive platform that enables seamless collaboration between GlobalDyne Industries and our network of 5,000+ suppliers worldwide.

## Portal Overview

### Access and Registration
- **Web-based Platform**: Accessible 24/7 from any device
- **Multi-language Support**: Available in 15 languages
- **Role-based Access**: Different permission levels for various user types
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: Integration with corporate identity systems

### User Roles
- **Supplier Administrator**: Full access to all supplier functions
- **Procurement Manager**: Order management and forecasting
- **Quality Manager**: Quality metrics and compliance reporting
- **Finance Contact**: Invoicing and payment status
- **Logistics Coordinator**: Shipping and delivery coordination

## Core Functionality

### Purchase Order Management

#### Order Processing Workflow
1. **PO Receipt**: Electronic purchase orders delivered instantly
2. **Acknowledgment**: Required within 24 hours of receipt
3. **Status Updates**: Real-time progress tracking
4. **Shipping Notification**: ASN (Advance Ship Notice) creation
5. **Receipt Confirmation**: Delivery verification and acceptance

#### Order Information Included
- Line item details with specifications
- Delivery dates and shipping instructions
- Quality requirements and certifications
- Payment terms and conditions
- Special handling instructions

### Forecasting and Planning

#### Demand Forecasting
- **Rolling Forecasts**: 13-week rolling demand projections
- **Seasonal Planning**: Holiday and peak season adjustments
- **New Product Introductions**: Launch planning and ramp schedules
- **End of Life**: Product discontinuation timelines

#### Collaborative Planning
- **Capacity Planning**: Supplier capacity vs. demand alignment
- **Raw Material Planning**: Upstream supply chain coordination
- **Risk Assessment**: Supply disruption identification and mitigation
- **Performance Reviews**: Quarterly business reviews

#### Quality Requirements
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA)**: Required for all shipments

### Financial Management

#### Invoice Processing
- **Electronic Invoicing**: EDI 810 or web portal submission
- **Three-way Matching**: PO, receipt, and invoice validation
- **Dispute Resolution**: Online dispute management system
- **Payment Tracking**: Real-time payment status visibility

#### Payment Terms
- **Standard Terms**: Net 45 days from receipt
- **Early Payment Discount**: 2/10 net 45 available
- **Dynamic Discounting**: Flexible early payment options
- **International Payments**: Multi-currency support

## Performance Monitoring

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

#### Delivery Performance
- **On-Time Delivery**: Target >95%
- **In-Full Delivery**: Target >98%
- **Perfect Order Rate**: Combines on-time, in-full, damage-free
- **Lead Time Performance**: Actual vs. committed lead times

#### Quality Performance
- **Defect Rate**: Target < 0.5%
- **First Pass Yield**: Target >99%
- **Customer Complaints**: Attributed to supplier quality
- **Corrective Action Responsiveness**: Time to resolution

#### Service Performance
- **Communication Responsiveness**: Response time to inquiries
- **Innovation Contribution**: New ideas and cost savings
- **Flexibility**: Ability to handle changes and rush orders
- **Compliance**: Adherence to policies and procedures

### Supplier Scorecards
- **Monthly Scorecards**: Detailed performance metrics
- **Quarterly Reviews**: Face-to-face or virtual meetings
- **Annual Assessments**: Comprehensive supplier evaluation
- **Improvement Plans**: Action items for performance enhancement
