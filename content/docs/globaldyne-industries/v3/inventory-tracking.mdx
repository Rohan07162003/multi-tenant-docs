# Inventory Tracking v3

GlobalDyne Industries' **third-generation inventory tracking platform** builds upon our proven systems with AI-driven demand prediction, blockchain-backed supply chain transparency, and autonomous warehouse robotics integration.

---

## Next-Gen Tracking Technologies

### RFID & Beyond
- **Ultra-High Frequency (UHF) Gen 3**: Faster reads, improved anti-collision
- **High Frequency (HF) NFC Integration**: Mobile device compatibility for field scanning
- **Passive Tags with Environmental Sensors**: Embedded temperature/humidity logging
- **Active Tags with LoRaWAN**: Long-range, low-power GPS tracking

### Smart Barcode Systems
- **Dynamic QR Codes**: Real-time data updates (expiry, batch, routing)
- **GS1 Digital Link**: Web-enabled product identifiers for richer information
- **Blockchain-Registered Batches**: Immutable provenance records
- **Augmented Reality Scanning**: Overlays inventory info during warehouse navigation

### IoT & Edge Devices
- **5G-Connected Sensors**: Millisecond latency for real-time control
- **Computer Vision Shelf Monitoring**: AI-based stock level detection
- **Vibration Sensors**: Detect mishandling during transit
- **Edge Gateways**: Local data processing to reduce cloud load
